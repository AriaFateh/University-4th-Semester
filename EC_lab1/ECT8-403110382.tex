Observations of the current waveform under different conditions showed that amplitude increases with an increasing air gap, while the waveform becomes smoother.

Increasing the air gap increases the magnetic reluctance of the circuit, forcing more flux to be supplied by the core's magnetizing inductance rather than leakage or eddy currents. This shifts the dominant behavior toward a higher-order LC resonance, which naturally filters out high-frequency noise and smooths the current waveform.

	Increasing the air gap drastically increases magnetic reluctance.ou need a larger magnetomotive force (MMF) (N⋅I). Since the number of turns (N) is fixed, you must increase the current. Because the core now behaves more like a resistive load than an ideal inductor at low frequencies, the voltage drop required to drive this current through the increased reluctance rises, enforces a higher input voltage.
