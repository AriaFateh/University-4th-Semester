\section{Problem 1}
$$ n_s = \frac{120 f_e}{p} \implies 1000 = \frac{120 \times 50}{p} \implies p = 6 \text{ poles} $$


\begin{itemize}
	\item \textbf{Stator magnetic field speed:} Equal to the synchronous speed, $n_s = \qty{1000}{rpm}$.
	\item \textbf{Rotor magnetic field speed:} The rotor magnetic field revolves at synchronous speed relative to the stator, so it is also \qty{1000}{rpm}. Relative to the rotor itself, it revolves at the slip speed ($n_{slip} = n_s - n_m$).
\end{itemize}


At No-Load:
$$ s_{nl} = \frac{n_s - n_{nl}}{n_s} = \frac{1000 - 980}{1000} = 0.02 $$
$$ f_{r,nl} = s_{nl} f_e = 0.02 \times 50 = \qty{1.0}{\Hz} $$

At Full-Load:
$$ s_{fl} = \frac{n_s - n_{fl}}{n_s} = 0.07 $$
$$ f_{r,fl} = s_{fl} f_e =\qty{3.5}{\Hz} $$


$$ SR = \frac{n_{nl} - n_{fl}}{n_{fl}} \times 100\% \approx 5.38\% $$

\section{Problem 2}


For a Y-connected motor, the phase voltage is:
$$ V_\phi = \frac{V_L}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{400}{\sqrt{3}} \approx \qty{230.94}{\V} $$
The synchronous angular speed is:
$$ \omega_s = \frac{4 \pi f_e}{p} = \qty{314.16}{\radian/\second} $$


The rotor impedance referred to the stator at $s = 0.05$ is:
$$ Z_2 = \frac{R_2}{s} + jX_2  = 1.36 + j0.18 \ \Omega $$
The parallel branch impedance (magnetizing reactance and rotor impedance):
$$ Z_p = \frac{jX_M \cdot Z_2}{jX_M + Z_2} \approx 1.258 + j0.404 \ \Omega $$
Total equivalent impedance:
$$ Z_{eq} = R_1 + jX_1 + Z_p = 1.336 + j0.584 \ \Omega $$
$$ Z_{eq} \approx 1.458 \angle 23.6^\circ \ \Omega $$
The line current (equal to phase current in Y-connection):
$$ \mathbf{I}_1 = \frac{V_\phi}{Z_{eq}} = \frac{230.94 \angle 0^\circ}{1.458 \angle 23.6^\circ} = 158.4 \angle -23.6^\circ \ \text{A} $$
Stator power factor:
$$ PF = \cos(23.6^\circ) = 0.916 \text{ lagging} $$


Stator copper loss:
$$ P_{scl} = 3 I_1^2 R_1 = 3 (158.4)^2 (0.078) \approx \qty{5871}{\W} = \qty{5.87}{\kilo\W} $$
Input power:
$$ P_{in} = \sqrt{3} V_L I_L \cos\theta= \qty{100.5}{\kilo\W} $$
Air-gap power:
$$ P_{ag} = P_{in} - P_{scl} - P_{core} = \qty{93.33}{\kilo\W} $$
Converted power (Mechanical power developed):
$$ P_{conv} = (1 - s)P_{ag} \approx \qty{88.66}{\kilo\W} $$
Output power:
$$ P_{out} = P_{conv} - P_{mech} =\qty{87.56}{\kilo\W} $$


Electromagnetic torque:
$$ T_e = \frac{P_{ag}}{\omega_s} \approx \qty{297.1}{\newton\meter} $$
Rotor speed:
$$ \omega_m = (1 - s)\omega_s = \qty{298.45}{\radian/\second} $$
Load torque:
$$ T_{load} = \frac{P_{out}}{\omega_m} \approx \qty{293.4}{\newton\meter} $$
Overall efficiency:
$$ \eta = \frac{P_{out}}{P_{in}} \approx 87.1\% $$


Using the Thevenin equivalent circuit:
$$ V_{th} \approx V_\phi \frac{X_M}{X_1 + X_M} \approx \qty{225.07}{\V} $$
$$ R_{th} \approx R_1 \left(\frac{X_M}{X_1 + X_M}\right)^2 \approx \qty{0.0741}{\Omega} $$
$$ X_{th} \approx X_1 = \qty{0.18}{\Omega} $$
The slip at maximum (pull-out) torque is:
$$ s_{max} = \frac{R_2}{\sqrt{R_{th}^2 + (X_{th} + X_2)^2}} \approx 0.185 $$
The pull-out torque is:
$$ T_{max} = \frac{3 V_{th}^2}{2 \omega_s \left[ R_{th} + \sqrt{R_{th}^2 + (X_{th} + X_2)^2} \right]} $$
$$ T_{max} \approx \qty{547.8}{\newton\meter} $$



For maximum torque to occur at starting, we need $s_{max} = 1$. This means the total rotor resistance referred to the stator ($R_{2,new}$) must equal the denominator of the $s_{max}$ formula:
$$ R_{2,new} = \sqrt{R_{th}^2 + (X_{th} + X_2)^2} = \qty{0.3675}{\Omega} $$
The external resistance referred to the stator is:
$$ R_{ext}' = R_{2,new} - R_2 = \qty{0.2995}{\Omega} $$
To find the actual physical external resistance to be added per phase to the rotor, we divide by the square of the turns ratio ($a^2$):
$$ R_{ext} = \frac{R_{ext}'}{a^2} \approx \qty{0.0749}{\Omega} \text{ per phase} $$
The value of the starting torque with this added resistance is simply the maximum torque computed above:
$$ T_{start} = T_{max} \approx \qty{547.8}{\newton\meter} $$

\section{Problem 3}
The synchronous speed in rpm and rad/s is:
\begin{align*}
	n_{sync} &= \frac{120f}{p} = \frac{120 \times 60}{4} = \SI{1800}{rpm} \\
	\omega_{sync} &= 1800 \times \frac{2\pi}{60} = \SI{188.5}{rad/s}
\end{align*}

Since the motor is Y-connected, the phase voltage is:
\begin{equation*}
	V_\phi = \frac{V_L}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{230}{\sqrt{3}} = \SI{132.79}{V}
\end{equation*}


To simplify the torque equations, we find the Thevenin voltage ($V_{th}$) and impedance ($Z_{th} = R_{th} + jX_{th}$) looking into the circuit from the rotor terminals.
\begin{align*}
	V_{th} &\approx V_\phi \left( \frac{X_M}{X_1 + X_M} \right)\approx \SI{128.79}{V} \\
	R_{th} &\approx R_1 \left( \frac{X_M}{X_1 + X_M} \right)^2 \approx \SI{0.320}{\Omega} \\
	X_{th} &= X_1 \parallel X_M = \SI{0.485}{\Omega}
\end{align*}

Since rotational losses are zero, the developed mechanical power ($P_{conv}$) equals the output power:
\begin{equation*}
	P_{conv} = P_{out} = \SI{7460}{W}
\end{equation*}
The load torque at full load ($s=0.04$) is:
\begin{equation*}
	T_{load} = \frac{P_{conv}}{\omega_m} = \frac{7460}{\omega_{sync}(1-s)} = \SI{41.22}{N.m}
\end{equation*}

The torque equation of the induction motor is given by:
\begin{equation*}
	T = \frac{3 V_{th}^2 \left(\frac{R_2}{s}\right)}{\omega_{sync} \left[ \left(R_{th} + \frac{R_2}{s}\right)^2 + (X_{th} + X_2)^2 \right]}
\end{equation*}
Let $x = \frac{R_2}{0.04}$. Solving this resulting quadratic equation for $x$ yields $x \approx 5.568$, which gives:
\begin{equation*}
	R_2 = \SI{0.223}{\Omega}
\end{equation*}

The slip at which maximum (pullout) torque occurs is:
\begin{equation*}
	s_{max} = \frac{R_2}{\sqrt{R_{th}^2 + (X_{th} + X_2)^2}}
\end{equation*}
\begin{equation*}
	s_{max} \approx 0.215
\end{equation*}

The equation for maximum induced torque is independent of $R_2$:
\begin{equation*}
	T_{max} = \frac{3 V_{th}^2}{2 \omega_{sync} \left[ R_{th} + \sqrt{R_{th}^2 + (X_{th} + X_2)^2} \right]}
\end{equation*}
\begin{equation*}
	T_{max}\approx \SI{97.38}{N.m}
\end{equation*}


The starting torque occurs when slip $s = 1.0$:
\begin{equation*}
	T_{start} = \frac{3 V_{th}^2 R_2}{\omega_{sync} \left[ (R_{th} + R_2)^2 + (X_{th} + X_2)^2 \right]}
\end{equation*}
\begin{equation*}
	T_{start} \approx \SI{46.52}{N.m}
\end{equation*}
